Games

Every game you own. Even the ones not downloaded.

Fenrir opens on your games: every one it finds on this PC, and the ones your libraries hold that are not downloaded yet. Open any of them to see what it is like before you install it.

  • DiscoverThe mods people play most for each of your games, from Thunderstore, GameBanana and Nexus Mods.

Mods

One click in. Every file back out.

Where a game has a mod scene, a mod goes in with one click, with what it needs and the loader set up for it. Removing it puts the game's folder back exactly as it was.

  • Where the mods areThunderstore for around 300 games, GameBanana for Half-Life 2 and Portal, and Nexus Mods searched in Fenrir and downloaded on its page. Any game takes a .zip, .7z or .rar.
  • Every original file comes backEach file a mod replaces waits in the game's folder and goes back when the mod goes, even if Fenrir was closed part-way. Nothing is deleted that Fenrir did not place unchanged.
  • Updates and setsUpdate all brings a game's Thunderstore mods up to date, each keeping its switch and place. Mod sets keep the mods that work together under a name.
  • Anti-cheat stays untouchedGames whose anti-cheat bans for changed files are shown and left alone, and a mod loader Fenrir downloads again must be the very file it got before.

Crash help

It names the mod. You play again.

When a modded game you started from Fenrir closes with an error, a notification says so at once. Fenrir reads what the game, its mod loader and Windows wrote, and names the mod most likely to blame.

  • Likely, or maybeA mod whose file is named in the crash is likely; one named further down may be. The game's own files and the loader are never blamed.
  • Take it out and playOne press takes the mod out and starts the game again. Go back to these brings the set you played with before.
  • The loaders it readsBepInEx, MelonLoader, Unity and Unreal games, Script Hook for GTA V, SKSE and crash loggers for Skyrim, and tModLoader.

Game servers

Host the game. It looks after itself.

Valheim, Palworld, 7 Days to Die, Terraria, GTA V through FiveM and Skyrim Together, beside your Minecraft worlds. Host a game's server asks for its few settings, then one press downloads it, sets it up and starts it.

  • One Servers sectionPick a server in the sidebar, and Dashboard, Console, Players, Server settings, Backups and Mods all show that one.
  • The same mods as your gameValheim's and 7 Days to Die's servers get your game's mods when they start; one you take off leaves the server too.
  • It keeps itself runningA crash starts it again (three in ten minutes and it waits for you), it comes back when Fenrir does, and it can restart daily and stop when nobody is on.
  • Backups of its worldOn a timer while the world changes, the newest twenty kept. A restore keeps the world as it was first, so it can be undone.
  • Who can join, in one lineGreen when friends anywhere can join, amber when only nearer ones can, with the one step that widens it: the firewall, the router's ports, a tunnel.

Minecraft

Minecraft worlds, too. With any modpack.

Minecraft is one of the games Fenrir hosts, with a few pages of its own. Start a world from a Modrinth or CurseForge pack, a server zip, plain Minecraft or Paper, and friends get the whole pack with Fenrir Connect.

  • Pack updates that test themselvesThe update backs up, installs and starts the new version once. If it crashes, stops or hangs, the old version comes back with the world.
  • Players, spelled rightA friend's name arrives with a Whitelist button. One player's inventory and position come back from a backup.
  • The DoctorIt checks Java, memory, ports, duplicate mods, crash suspects and more, and each finding has a button that fixes it.

Sites and apps

Anything on this PC. Online with one switch.

Sites & apps puts three kinds of thing online: a folder Fenrir serves as a website, an app or project Fenrir starts with its own command, or a port a program on this PC already serves.

  • A folder, safely servedOnly its own files: no folder listings, and nothing hidden like .git or .env.
  • An app with its commandFenrir starts it in its folder with PORT set, keeps what it prints in a log, and stops everything it started.
  • Three ways to reach itA free public address from Cloudflare with no account, your own domain with a Cloudflare tunnel token, or your home network.
  • Off until you switch it onWindows keeps the domain token encrypted, and quitting Fenrir takes everything offline.

Friends

One page per friend. One link each.

Type a name and Fenrir makes that friend a key, a link and a message to paste anywhere. The Friends page then shows each friend at a glance: around or not, which of your games they have ready, and their answer for game night.

  • A link to one gameInvite a friend… on a game's page makes a link that opens on that game, and their page shows whether they opened it.
  • Mods that travelShare a game and their Fenrir Connect gets the same mods from where they are published. A mod you made comes from your PC, looked through first.
  • Yours to changeRename a friend, give them a new key, or turn their link off, with Undo. With the address relay on, their apps follow a new address by themselves.

Game nights

Plan the night. It starts itself.

Plan a night once or every week, on any of your servers. Discord hears about it an hour before, the server starts ten minutes early, and it stops once it has been empty for half an hour.

  • Tonight, on the Games pageOn the day the night waits at the top of Games: what, when, where and who is in, with Start now and tell friends.
  • In every friend's timeDiscord and the invite page show the time in each friend's own zone, and their apps say how long until it starts.
  • Two in one eveningEach night starts its own server and stops it once it empties. A server you started yourself is never stopped by a night.

Ask Fenrir and AI apps

Tell it what you want. Say yes to what it does.

Type a sentence into the command palette (Ctrl+K) and choose Ask Fenrir. It lists every change as a ticked step, and nothing happens until you press Do it.

  • The everyday, with no AIStart or stop a server, who is on, back up, plan a game night, invite a friend, why a game crashed, install or update mods. The AI you picked answers the rest.
  • AI apps, through MCPClaude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor and other apps can look at what runs once you switch them on, and change things once you allow it.
  • Your yes, in Fenrir's own dialogEach change waits for a Windows dialog an app cannot answer. Inviting, installing a mod and putting a site online always ask.

Mashups

Up to four games. Built with you.

Pick the game you play in, up to three more, and what each brings: world and maps, player and movement, physics, weapons, enemies, vehicles, building, music, look, rules or multiplayer. A game you have not downloaded can join too.

  • A starter that already loadsEach project starts with a brief, a starter that builds and loads into the game you play in, and other modders' know-how for its engines.
  • The builder is the AI you pickLM Studio or Ollama on this PC, or OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini with your own key, which Windows keeps encrypted.
  • It asks before it actsBefore it builds, puts the mod into the game or plays it, unless you say Always for this project. Fenrir Link can answer from your phone.
  • Saves first, friends lastA mashup's first Play keeps the game's saves, and nothing goes to friends before Fenrir has looked it through.

A way back

Whatever it changes, it can put back.

Settings lists everything Fenrir changed on this PC, from its files in your games to firewall rules, router ports and what starts with Windows, each with its way back.

  • Backups that are checkedAn old archive goes only once a newer one has been read back whole, and a restore is rehearsed each month.
  • Updates that are signedEach update is checked against a signature only Fenrir's publisher can make, and a build that does not start gives way to the one you had.
  • The DoctorIt finds what is wrong (ports, memory, tunnels, friends on an old link, backups that did not upload) with a fix for each.
