Step 1 · the link

It starts with a link.

Your host sends you a link of your own. It opens a page that greets you by name, says whether the server is up and which mods the game needs, and has one button: Download Fenrir Setup, with your key already inside.

  • Nothing to pasteFenrir Setup reads your key by itself, and Fenrir Connect connects on its own.
  • A link to one gameA link to a game opens Fenrir Connect straight on that game, so Get ready and Join are one press each.
  • Game night, in your timeThe page your link opens shows the next game night in your own time zone.

Step 2 · Get ready

The same mods. Your own copy.

Get ready puts your host's mods into your own copy of the game, at the exact versions your host plays with, fetched from where their makers publish them.

  • From where the mods are publishedThunderstore, GitHub and GameBanana come straight to your PC. A Nexus Mods file you download on its page, in two clicks, as Nexus asks.
  • A mod your host madeIt comes from your host's PC, and Fenrir Connect checks it before it goes in.
  • Your own mods stay yoursFenrir Connect leaves them alone, and Remove the mods puts every file of your game back.

Step 3 · Join

One button. You are in.

Join starts the game and points it at your host's server: Valheim connects straight to the first of your host's addresses that answers, and FiveM opens through its own link.

  • Mods only while you playSome games, like Elden Ring with Seamless Co-op, get their mods only while the game runs, and they come out when it closes.
  • Your game, as it wasRemove the mods puts every file back. Uninstalling Fenrir Connect takes its mods out of every game first.
  • Up to date by itselfFenrir Connect updates itself from your host and, with your host's address relay on, follows them to a new address.

Minecraft packs, too

A whole modpack. Installed for you.

When your host shares a Minecraft world, Fenrir Connect puts the whole pack into TLauncher, sets the memory for your PC, and joins the world as soon as the game loads.

  • Only what is missingEach file is checked, mostly downloaded straight from CurseForge and Mojang. A download that drops picks up where it stopped.
  • Repair and RemoveRepair checks every file against your host's; Remove puts your own folders back.

The Tools page

Something off? Tools has it.

For the evening the game will not start, the server will not answer, or your host asks what is wrong. Every tool answers right where you pressed it.

  • Test the connectionWhether your host's Fenrir answers from this PC, how fast, and who can fix it when it does not.
  • A summary for your hostThis PC, your mods and the last error in one message to paste. Your key is never in it.
  • For a Minecraft worldSend the game's log to your host's Fenrir, which reads it for them, and check every file of the pack against your host's, fixing whatever differs.

A note on Windows

Windows may ask. Press Run anyway.

Fenrir is not signed by a big publisher, so SmartScreen may say Windows protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway. You see it once.

What you need

A Windows PC. Your game. A link.

Windows 10 or 11

Fenrir Connect installs for your Windows account only, with no administrator prompt.

Your own copy of the game

Fenrir Connect finds it on your PC, and a game you do not have yet opens on its Steam page. For a Minecraft pack, TLauncher.

The link from your host

It carries your key, so nothing needs pasting. No account, and no password.

Got a link? Open it. That’s all.

The page your host’s link opens has everything, with your key inside. No link yet? Ask your host to invite you from Fenrir's Friends page.
