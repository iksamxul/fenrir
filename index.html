Every game you own

Mods in one click. Every file back.

Fenrir finds your games on Steam, Epic, GOG, Ubisoft, EA and Xbox, and the ones you own but have not downloaded yet. Mods go in with one click and come out whole.

  • One click where a game has modsThunderstore for around 300 games, GameBanana, and Nexus Mods searched right in Fenrir. Any game also takes a .zip, .7z or .rar.
  • Every original file comes backFenrir keeps each file a mod replaces and puts it back when the mod goes, even if Fenrir was closed part-way.
  • It names the mod that crashedWhen a modded game closes with an error, Fenrir names the mod most likely to blame, with Take it out and play.
  • Anti-cheat stays safeGames that ban for changed files are shown and never touched.

Sites and apps

Your own projects. Online with one switch.

Serve a folder as a website, run an app or project with its own command, or share a port a program already uses. Each one gets a free public address, your own domain, or stays on your home network.

  • A free address, no accountA Cloudflare quick tunnel gives each one an https address, with no router changes.
  • Your own domainPaste a Cloudflare tunnel's token once, and each site answers at its own name.
  • Nothing online until you say soEach item has its own switches, and quitting Fenrir takes them all offline.

Friends

A link for each friend. They press Join.

Type a name and Fenrir makes that friend a link of their own. It greets them and has Fenrir Setup with their key inside. Fenrir Connect gets their copy of the game ready with the same mods, and Join puts them in your game.

  • Mods from where they are publishedThunderstore, GitHub and GameBanana straight to their PC. A mod you made comes from yours, checked first.
  • Their own mods stay theirsFenrir Connect leaves them alone, and Remove the mods puts their game back exactly as it was.
  • You see who is readyThe Friends page shows each friend's games, when they were last on and their answer for game night.

Game nights

Plan the night. It starts itself.

Pick a server, a day and a time, once or every week. Friends answer I’m in from their apps or phones, Fenrir starts the server ten minutes before, and stops it once it has been empty for half an hour.

  • The right time for every friendDiscord and the invite page show it in each friend’s own time zone, and their apps say how long until it starts.
  • Tonight, one press awayOn the day it waits at the top of Games: Start now and tell friends.
  • On any of your serversValheim, Palworld, a Minecraft world: each night starts and stops its own.

Safe to run

Free, private, and always a way back.

No account, no ads, no analytics. What leaves your PC is what you switch on, and every change Fenrir makes comes with its way back.

Free, with no account

Fenrir, Fenrir Connect and Fenrir Link cost nothing, and there is nothing to sign up for.

No analytics

What leaves your PC: update checks, the mod sites you install from, the tunnels you switch on and the AI you choose.

Signed updates

Every update is checked against a signature only Fenrir’s publisher can make. If a new build does not start, the one you had comes back.

Every file comes back

Removing mods puts each game’s own files back, and Settings lists everything Fenrir changed on this PC, each with its way back.

Backups that are checked

Every archive is read back before an old one goes, and a restore is rehearsed each month.

Your account, no admin prompt

It installs for your Windows account only. Windows asks only when you choose, such as letting friends through the firewall.

Questions

Good to know before tonight.

Is Fenrir really free?

Yes. Fenrir, Fenrir Connect and Fenrir Link are free, with no account and no ads. The free tunnels it uses for friends cost nothing either.

Which games can it host a server for?

Valheim, Palworld, 7 Days to Die, Terraria, GTA V through FiveM and Skyrim Together, and Minecraft worlds with any NeoForge, Forge, Fabric or Quilt pack, plain Minecraft or Paper. Mods work for many more games than servers do.

What do my friends need?

Windows 10 or 11, their own copy of the game, and the link you send them. For a Minecraft pack, TLauncher.

Can mods break my game for good?

No. Remove all from the game puts back every file a mod replaced and takes out every file Fenrir placed. Games whose anti-cheat bans for changed files are never touched.

Can I close Fenrir and keep my servers running?

Yes. Closing the window only hides Fenrir: servers, tunnels and the phone remote keep running, and its icon by the clock opens it again.
