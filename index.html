Every game you own. Even the ones not downloaded.

Mashups

Up to four games. Each brings its part.

Pick the game you play in and up to three more, then what each one brings: its world, its weapons, its vehicles, its building, its music. Fenrir sets up the project, and the AI you choose builds it with you. It writes the code, builds it, asks before it plays, and reads the logs to fix what broke. Each project starts with what other modders learned: a guide for every game's engine, their notes on these games, and the simplest way to bring each one in. Mashups that others made, like SkyCraft, install with one click.

Ask Fenrir

Say what you want. It shows you first.

Press Ctrl+K and type it the way you would say it: start Valheim, who is on, a game night on Friday at 8, why did Lethal Company crash, install BiggerLobby. Fenrir understands the everyday ones on its own, and the AI you picked answers the rest. It tells you what it looked at, and every change waits for your Do it.

Claude Code, Claude Desktop and other AI apps on your PC can use Fenrir too, once you switch it on. They look first; with your Allow they start servers, back up and plan game nights. Nothing opens to the Internet, and every change is on the timeline.

When a mod breaks a game

It crashed? Fenrir names the mod.

When a modded game you started from Fenrir closes with an error, Fenrir tells you at once: it reads what the game and its mod loader wrote, and names the mod most likely to blame. Take it out and play again in one press. Mod sets keep the mods that work together under a name, and Fenrir remembers the mods each Play used, so the set from Tuesday is one press away.

  • BepInEx, MelonLoader, Unity, Unreal
  • Skyrim, GTA V, Terraria

Hosting

Your sites and apps. Online from this PC.

Serve a folder as a website, run an app or project with its own command, or share a port a program already uses. Each one gets a free public address, your own domain through Cloudflare, or stays on this PC and your home network. Nothing goes online until you switch it on, and the game servers and your Minecraft world are on the same list.

  • A free address, no account
  • Your own domain
  • No router changes

Playing together

One PC. Everyone’s game.

The server runs on your PC, and a free tunnel gives it an address your friends can reach from anywhere. Each friend gets a link of their own, so you always know who is in, and Fenrir Connect gets their copy of the game ready with the same mods. You see which friends are ready for each game you share. Plan a game night on any of your servers: Fenrir starts it ahead, tells everyone, and stops it once it has been empty for half an hour.

  • No router changes
  • A key per friend
  • Minecraft, Valheim, Palworld and more

Fenrir Connect

Friends install nothing by hand.

Fenrir Connect puts the whole pack into TLauncher, sets the memory for their PC and updates in one click. About 20 MB comes from your PC. CurseForge and Mojang send the rest. Its Dashboard shows them the world first, and its Tools page tests the connection for them.

Safety

There is always a way back.

Set backups to every hour and Fenrir keeps a month of history. Every archive is checked, a backup that missed a file never pushes out a whole one, and a restore is rehearsed each month. If an updated pack will not start, the old version comes back. And Settings lists everything Fenrir changed on this PC, from files in your games to firewall rules, each with its way back.
