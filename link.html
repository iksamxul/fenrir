Linking a world

Two codes. One app.

Every person has a code of their own, and the code is the key. Scan it once and the phone keeps it in its keychain, so there is no account and no password.

  1. Your own server. Open Fenrir on the PC that hosts. The Fenrir Link card on the Dashboard shows your code.
  2. A friend's world. Open Fenrir Connect. Your code is on the You card.
  3. Your phone. Open Fenrir Link, press +, then Scan a code. That is the whole setup.

Safety

A passkey, not a password.

Kept in the keychain

Each link lives in the phone's own keychain: the Android Keystore or the iPhone Keychain. There is no account to sign in to.

Only over a secure address

Fenrir answers your server's code only over https, through Cloudflare or Tailscale. A plain address never carries it.

Yours to replace

Replace the key in Fenrir and the old code stops working at once. The phone says so and asks for the new one.

For iPhone

Apple only installs App Store apps directly, so this takes a computer once.

  1. On a computer, install AltStore (altstore.io) or Sideloadly (sideloadly.io).
  2. Download FenrirLink.ipa and open it with either one. They sign it with your own Apple ID.
  3. On a free Apple ID the app needs a refresh every 7 days. AltStore does that by itself.
