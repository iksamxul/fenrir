Linking

Two codes. One app.

Every person has a code of their own, and the code is the key. Scan it once and the phone keeps it, so there is no account and no password.

  1. Your own servers. Open Fenrir on the PC that hosts. The Fenrir Link card on the Dashboard shows your code.
  2. A friend's games. Open Fenrir Connect. Your code is on the You page.
  3. Your phone. Open Fenrir Link, press +, then Scan a code. On an iPhone the Camera does it: scan, then Share, then Add to Home Screen. That is the whole setup.

A Minecraft world, on four tabs

The whole console. Every tool.

A Minecraft world you host gets the four tabs of Fenrir's own pages. Nothing to set up: it is all in the code you scanned.

  • DashboardThe world's state, TPS, tick time and memory, a chart of the last half hour, and friends who want to play or need a hand.
  • PlayersWho is on and for how long, a message to one of them, a kick, and the whitelist to add to or take from.
  • ConsoleThe server's console, live, with chat, issues and Fenrir's own lines a tap apart, quick commands and a command line.
  • ToolsBack up now and the recent backups, save the world, make it day, the note to friends, and who can start the world.

Safety

A passkey, not a password.

Kept to yourself

In the app, each link lives in the phone's own keychain: the Android Keystore or the iPhone Keychain. On the home screen, the page's address is the key. There is no account to sign in to.

Only over a secure address

Fenrir answers your server's code only over https, through Cloudflare or Tailscale. A plain address never carries it.

Yours to replace

Replace the key in Fenrir and the old code stops working at once. The phone says so and asks for the new one.

Get Fenrir Link

Free on Android and on iPhone.

For Android

Android 8 and later. Install it straight from the download.

  1. Download FenrirLink.apk on the phone.
  2. When Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Open the file and press Install.
  4. Then turn Install unknown apps back off for your browser: Settings, Apps, your browser.

For iPhone

Straight from Safari: no App Store, no computer, nothing to sign.

  1. Point the iPhone’s Camera at your code (Fenrir’s Dashboard, or the You page in Fenrir Connect) and tap the link.
  2. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Fenrir Link sits on your home screen and opens full screen, straight to your world. Your host’s Fenrir keeps it up to date.

Prefer the full app, with every world in one place? FenrirLink.ipa installs through AltStore (altstore.io) or Sideloadly (sideloadly.io) on a computer, signed with your own Apple ID.
